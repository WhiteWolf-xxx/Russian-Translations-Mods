XuanZeBuFeng: The origin of this mod can be traced back to when I first started playing Rain World.
XuanZeBuFeng: While watching a video once, I noticed a modded Slugcat had added a snowy Outer Expense.
XuanZeBuFeng: But I didn't know which modded Slugcat it was at the time, and later I just dropped it.
XuanZeBuFeng: When I had just become a map modder, I amused myself by casually changing the settings of a room.
XuanZeBuFeng: I created a snowy Outer Expense room.
XuanZeBuFeng: Later, whenever I had free time, I tinkered a bit, changing the Outer Expense to a snowy scene.
XuanZeBuFeng: Then I realized I really liked the snowy Outer Expense, so I thought, "Why not just make it into a mod?"
XuanZeBuFeng: This is the origin of this mod.
