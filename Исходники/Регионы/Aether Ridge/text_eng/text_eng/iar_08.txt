IAR_08
These are detailed work plans to retrofit the carriages in a telescopic rail station with additional parts.

Freight systems like these were a popular and scalable solution for hauling cargo onto service utility decks.

The parts in question are refillable hoppers and direct line sensors.

The sensors would simultaneously shut the newly added hoppers and seal the carriage for transport.

At least - that's what they were intended to accomplish. I don't know if these were actually built.

Anyway...this particular system is very large - it could even reach the ground when fully extended!
