You have seen it little one.
Thousands upon thousands of flickering golden stars swimming to no end.
The great expanse, silent, yet chanting to all that`s living on this plane.
We followed as you did - curious, hopefull and desperate.
And yet... what has it brought us?
Only piles of corpses covered in dust, snow and rust.
We live on a rotten skeleton of times almost forgotten.
I pray that whatever comes after us will not repeat the same mistakes.
